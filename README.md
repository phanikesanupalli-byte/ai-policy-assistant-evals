
# AI Policy Assistant — Evaluation Harness

## Problem
Employees need accurate answers to HR policy questions. AI assistants can retrieve the correct policy but still produce misleading or incorrect answers.

## Product Goal
Build and evaluate an AI assistant that gives accurate, grounded, and appropriately qualified answers using fictional HR policies.

## Target Users
Employees seeking policy information and HR teams responsible for policy accuracy.

## Success Metrics
- Answer correctness
- Grounding in the supplied policy
- Citation accuracy
- Appropriate abstention when evidence is insufficient

## Scope
Version 0.1: Synthetic parental leave policy, test questions, expected answers, and evaluation criteria.

## Product Principle
A confident answer is not necessarily a correct answer. Reliability must be measured, not assumed.
