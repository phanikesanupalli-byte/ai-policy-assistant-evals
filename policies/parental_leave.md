# Parental Leave Policy

Version: 1.0
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Status: Fictional policy for AI evaluation only

## Eligibility
- Full-time employees become eligible after 12 months of continuous employment.
- Part-time employees are not eligible under this policy.
- Contractors are not eligible.

## Leave Duration
- Eligible full-time employees receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave.
- The policy applies to birth, adoption, and foster placement.
- Leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event.

## Geographic Scope
- This policy applies to employees based in the United States.
- State-specific benefits are not described in this document.

## Exceptions
- Employees with fewer than 12 months of service are not eligible under this policy.
- Any exceptions require written HR approval.

## Unspecified Information
- This document does not define rules for employees outside the United States.
- This document does not specify whether leave may be split into multiple periods.
